# User content and commercial operations / 用户内容与商业运营边界

**This document explains the intended licensing boundary. It is not a live, accepted Terms of Service, Privacy Policy or an enforceable authorization from users.** Before launching user accounts, a public feed or paid promotion, publish separate user-facing agreements, have them reviewed and obtain applicable consent.

## 用户创作内容

- Users should retain the rights they hold in their independently created photos, tasting notes, bar reviews, recipes and posts.
- Hosting, display, moderation, content removal, in-app recommendations and user-initiated cross-platform sharing will require a clear, appropriately scoped license in the future Terms of Service; do not claim a blanket assignment of ownership.
- User content should not automatically become MPL-2.0 simply by being stored on the platform. Contributors who intentionally commit a file to this source repository are separately subject to the contribution policy.
- Photos, music, logos, recipes copied from others, and uploaded promotional assets still require their own authorization.

## 商业广告与合作

- Paid/promoted placements, sponsorships and merchant cooperation may be offered commercially; MPL-2.0 does not require the commercial prices, private customer databases, contacts or marketing contracts to be published.
- Clearly mark advertisements and sponsored posts in product UI and publishing/export flows; providing code under MPL-2.0 is not permission to hide sponsorship disclosures.
- Access to map SDKs, API keys, provider terms and upstream POI datasets must be separately obtained. Do not redistribute licensed third-party map tiles or private location records under this repository's license.

## 上线前的法律与安全任务

Account lifecycle, data deletion, storage retention, content moderation/reporting/appeals, privacy permissions, location consent, photo storage, sponsored content disclosure, and platform-specific terms are **not yet delivered** by these licensing documents. Confirm applicable rules before commercial launch.
